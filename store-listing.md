## Detailed Description

### Fiddy Send — WA Bulk Sender

Send personalized bulk messages to your customers directly from your browser — no API, no coding, no extra software.

### Why Fiddy Send?

- **📊 Import from Excel, CSV, or VCF** — Upload your contact list in one click
- **🏷️ Personalized messages** — Use our `{name}`, `{phone}`, `{company}` placeholders to fill in details automatically from your spreadsheet columns
- **🛡️ Anti-ban safe pacing** — Adjustable delays between messages keep your WhatsApp account safe
- **🖼️ Send media** — Attach images, brochures, or documents to your messages
- **🧪 Safe Test Mode** — Preview messages before sending anything live
- **📋 Full delivery report** — Know exactly what was sent, to whom, and when
- **🌙 Dark & light themes** — Beautiful purple/cyan interface

### Who is it for?

- Small business owners sending customer promotions
- Real estate agents updating buyers on new properties
- E-commerce stores sending order updates
- Community groups announcing events
- Anyone currently copy-pasting the same message to every contact

### How it works

1. Open WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) and log in
2. Click the Fiddy Send icon in your browser toolbar
3. Import your contacts (Excel/CSV) and write your message
4. Add personalization placeholders like {name}
5. Click Send — Fiddy Send handles the rest

### Important Notes

- Fiddy Send automates your own WhatsApp Web session in your browser
- Anti-ban pacing is enabled by default to protect your account
- Follow WhatsApp's Terms of Service: send only to contacts who have opted in
- Includes an unsubscribe/opt-out instruction option in every message (recommended)
