# Privacy Policy — Fiddy Send (WA Bulk Sender)

**Last updated:** September 11, 2026

Fiddy Send is a browser extension developed by **Fidean Technologies Limited** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the Fiddy Send browser extension.

## Information We Collect

### What the Extension Accesses

The Fiddy Send extension operates entirely within your browser and interacts with WhatsApp Web (`web.whatsapp.com`). The extension:

1. **Reads contact data you import** — Names and phone numbers from Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, or VCF files that you explicitly upload into the extension.
2. **Reads WhatsApp Web UI elements** — To send messages on your behalf (search bar, send button, chat area).
3. **Stores locally in your browser** — Message templates and settings are saved in `chrome.storage` on your device.

### What We DO NOT Collect

- We do **not** read your WhatsApp messages, contacts, or conversations.
- We do **not** access your browser history, bookmarks, passwords, or any other data outside the extension.
- We do **not** transmit your contact data to our servers unless you explicitly use the Chatdesk Sync feature (which requires your confirmation).
- We do **not** use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertisements.

## How We Use Information

### License Validation

When the extension checks your license status, it sends your **license key** to our server. No personal data is included in this request.

### License Key & Email

If you purchase a paid plan (Starter, Pro, or Unlimited), we store:
- Your email address (for billing and license activation)
- Your license key
- Payment transaction reference

This data is stored securely and is not shared with third parties except Paystack (our payment processor).

### Chatdesk Sync (Opt-In Only)

If you choose to sync contacts to Fiddy Chatdesk CRM, we temporarily receive the contact data you explicitly send. This action requires your manual confirmation each time.

## Data Storage & Security

- License data is stored in an encrypted database on our secured server.
- Payment processing is handled entirely by **Paystack** (paystack.com) — we never store your card details.
- Message templates and settings stored in `chrome.storage` remain on your device.

## Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Paystack (paystack.com) | Payment processing | Email, transaction amount, reference |
| GitHub (optional update check) | Extension auto-updates | None |
| WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) | Message sending | Messages you compose and send |

## Data Retention

- License information is retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days.
- Synced contacts are retained until you delete them via the Chatdesk dashboard.
- You may request deletion of your data by emailing privacy@fideantech.com.

## Your Rights

- You can uninstall the extension at any time — all local data is removed automatically.
- You can request a copy of or deletion of your license/contact data by emailing privacy@fideantech.com.
- You can disable Chatdesk Sync in the extension settings.

## Children's Privacy

Fiddy Send is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
